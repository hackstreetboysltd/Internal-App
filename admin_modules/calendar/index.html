About this Module

Admin console for the shared month calendar. Review and approve events and meetings, including join links, agendas, and post-meeting minutes.

Key Actions

  • Browse events and meetings on the monthly calendar.
  • Add dated events with a location or virtual link.
  • Schedule meetings with a time, join URL, agenda, and minutes.
  • Approve or reject pending events and meetings from a selected day.
